STM32F103C6T6: why use it?

A low-cost 72 MHz Cortex-M3 microcontroller in a 48-pin package. It is a smaller-memory sibling of the popular STM32F103C8T6 used on Blue Pill boards. See what the name means, what is inside, and whether it fits your project.
