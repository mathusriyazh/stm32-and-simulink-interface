How to choose the right board for your application

Do not pick a board by name or price alone. Start from what the project must do, then match it to a board. Follow the five steps, then use the board finder to see ranked matches.

4. Common mistakes

  • Buying the fastest board "just in case". It costs more, uses more power and is harder to set up.
  • Forgetting the pins. List every sensor and actuator, then check in STM32CubeMX that each peripheral has a free pin.
  • Ignoring voltage levels. STM32 pins are 3.3 V; many sensors and modules are 5 V.
  • Choosing a board with no debugger. A built-in ST-LINK saves hours of guessing.
  • Not leaving headroom. Keep about 20 % spare Flash, RAM and CPU time for future features.
  • Treating the dev board as the product. Prototype on a board, then move the chip to your own PCB.
